Filter tips

A quick, opinionated cheat-sheet for building a good shortlist. Skim it once — then use the column headers to filter.

1Start with a solid quality floor
Rating ≥ 4.5 · Reviews ≥ 50. Anything with fewer than 10 reviews is statistically noise.
2Trust the distribution, not the average
A 4.8 with 12% one-stars is worse than a 4.6 with 1% one-stars. Set Min 5★ ≥ 85% and Max 1–2★ ≤ 5%.
3Pick category by how you want to work
C1 turnkey & predictable · C2 best value · C3 design-led & personal · C4 signature luxury. (Full pros/cons in Categories guide.)
4Match volume to your tolerance for waiting
Bespoke / Moderate = more attention, longer waits. High / Massive = faster, more standardized.
5Use "Own Factory" for modular projects
Kitchens & wardrobes → prefer Yes (timelines + warranty). Pure on-site carpentry → No is fine. Be cautious with Outsourced.
6Filter by your zone, then expand
Start with your zone for fast site visits. Expand to adjacent zones if your shortlist drops below 5–10 studios.
7Treat "Ad" badges as paid placement
It's a marketing signal, not a quality signal. Hover the badge to see why. Judge them by rating distribution like everyone else.
8Sort, then hide
Click ⇅ on Rating or Reviews to sort. Use the eye icon (👁) to hide studios you've ruled out — your hidden list persists.
Recommended starter combo: Rating ≥ 4.5 · Reviews ≥ 50 · Min 5★ ≥ 80% · Max 1–2★ ≤ 10% · 1–2 zones near you. Then layer Category & Own Factory.

The 4 categories — explained

Every studio in this directory is placed into one of four tiers based on operating model, scale, pricing power, and design depth. Pick the tier that matches how you want to work, then filter further by zone, budget, and review quality.

C1 · AggregatorsHigh-Volume Tech Platforms & Aggregators
Venture-funded, tech-led brands operating at scale across multiple cities. Standardized packages, in-house factories, large pools of project-assigned designers, branded showrooms, and structured warranties.
Budget: ₹6L – ₹25L+Volume: 100 – 300+ /yrTimeline: 45 – 90 days
e.g., Livspace, DesignCafe, HomeLane, Bonito Designs, Interior Company

Pros

  • Fixed pricing & written timelines
  • Branded warranties (often 10 years)
  • Showroom experiences & 3D walkthroughs
  • EMI / financing options available
  • Process consistency & escalation paths

Cons

  • Less personalised, designer rotation common
  • Limited material / vendor flexibility
  • Active upsell pressure on add-ons
  • Brand premium over similar-quality C2/C3
  • Templated design language
C2 · Mid-PremiumOrganized Mid-to-Large Mid-Premium Players
Established Bangalore-based design-build firms with in-house teams and their own (or partnered) manufacturing. Mature processes without the franchise feel of C1 — usually a single principal/founder still actively involved.
Budget: ₹4L – ₹15LVolume: 25 – 150 /yrTimeline: 60 – 120 days
e.g., Blue Interiors, Asense, Ace Interiors, Technowood, Vivista

Pros

  • Best balance of process + personalisation
  • Local accountability — founder reachable
  • Stable senior designers across the project
  • Better price-to-quality than aggregators
  • Reasonable warranty & after-sales

Cons

  • Quality varies with assigned project lead
  • Smaller / no flagship showrooms
  • Limited tech tooling vs C1 (3D, apps)
  • Slower response than aggregators
  • Capacity constraints in peak season
C3 · BoutiqueBoutique & Bespoke Studios
Small principal-led studios where the founding designer is hands-on across every project. Tight teams, custom carpentry partners, strong design POV — but limited bandwidth.
Budget: ₹3L – ₹12LVolume: 8 – 50 /yrTimeline: 75 – 150 days
e.g., Gothra Studio, Asher Spaces, White Sketch, Butterfly Interior Design

Pros

  • Direct access to the principal designer
  • Highly personalised, no templates
  • Material & vendor flexibility
  • Stronger, distinctive design POV
  • Negotiable scope — pay only for what you need

Cons

  • Limited bandwidth — waitlists common
  • Process less standardized; expect ambiguity
  • Warranty depends on the individual
  • Execution can be slower
  • Larger upfront advances — vendor risk
C4 · LuxuryHigh-End Custom Luxury Designers
Architect/designer-led luxury practices working on a small number of large-budget homes. Heavy emphasis on bespoke design, imported finishes, and full project management — often with an interior architect leading the work.
Budget: ₹25L – ₹2Cr+Volume: 4 – 20 /yrTimeline: 6 – 18 months
e.g., Habitart Solutions, architect-led practices, premium villa specialists

Pros

  • Architect-grade design & spatial planning
  • Bespoke joinery & imported finishes
  • Full project management included
  • Distinctive, magazine-worthy outcomes
  • Strong lifestyle / brand alignment

Cons

  • 3–10× the cost of comparable C1/C2 work
  • Long timelines (6–18 months typical)
  • Strict design philosophy — less flexibility
  • Not suitable for < ₹25L projects
  • Limited availability — by referral often
How to choose: If you want predictable pricing and a turnkey experience, start with C1. If you want a better price-quality balance with a real local team, start with C2. If design quality and personalisation matter more than process, choose C3. If budget is open and you want a signature home, go to C4.
